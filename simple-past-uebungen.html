Remember

  • Regular verbs: infinitive + -ed (play → played, live → lived, study → studied, stop → stopped)
  • Irregular verbs: special forms – learn them by heart (go → went, see → saw)
  • Negatives: didn't + infinitive (I didn't go.) – but: wasn't / weren't
  • Questions: Did + subject + infinitive? (Did you go?) – but: Was / Were …?
  • With a question word: question word + did + subject + infinitive? (Where did you go?) – but: Why were they late?
